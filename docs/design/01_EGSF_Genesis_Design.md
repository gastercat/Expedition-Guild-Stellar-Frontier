## 0. Summary

- EG:SF began as a long-term multiplayer modpack for friends.
- The core identity became Create guild infrastructure + RPG role division + Ad Astra space expedition.
- The pack should not be treated as a random mod collection.
- Create is infrastructure, RPG is role division, and Ad Astra is the mid/late-game expedition goal.
- Create must remain 1.20.1-0.5.1.j and not Create 6.x.
